# How to use ProFocus

ProFocus runs on one loop: **season → week → day → focus → shutdown → review**. Each step takes a few minutes, and each one feeds the next.

## Once: set up (10 minutes)

1. **Settings → Account**: sign in with Google, then **Connect calendar**. Your Google events appear on Today and Week as grey "G" blocks, and every plan works around them.
2. **Settings → AI coach**: choose Anthropic (Claude), paste your Console key, then **Test → Save**.
3. **Settings → Your data → Add from file…**: load a prefill file if you have one. It only adds things; nothing you already have changes.
4. **Seasons (5)**: start a season if one isn't running. A season is 12 weeks with a theme and the 3–4 goals that get your time. Everything else waits for the next season.

## Every Monday: plan the week (10 minutes)

**Week (2) → Plan my week.**

1. **Last week.** Read the numbers. Press **Draft suggestions** to have the coach propose three priorities from your season goals and last week's results. Write one honest line in each reflection box.
2. **This week's three.** Choose three priorities, each linked to a season goal. Use **Use as priority** to take a coach suggestion, or type your own.
3. **Protect time.** Turn each priority into a task on a specific day, or press **Add all three**.

The Seasons screen strip fills in for each week you plan. That strip is your accountability check.

## Every morning: plan the day (2 minutes)

**Today (1) → Plan my day.**

1. **Triage.** Tick the inbox and overdue tasks that belong to *today*. The ones you leave stay in the inbox. **Only what you tick gets scheduled**, so your day contains exactly the tasks you chose.
2. **Highlight.** Pick the one thing that makes today a win.
3. **Block it.** Press **Schedule everything**. Tasks are placed in priority order (`!high` first) into the gaps around your calendar.

Then press **Coach, read my day** for a two-sentence check on what's protected, what has no slot, and what to do first.

**Setting priorities.** On any task, click its title and set Priority. In quick-add, type `!high`, `!med` or `!low`. High-priority tasks sort first and are scheduled first.

## During the day: work in focus blocks

- **▶ next to a task** starts a focus session on it.
- **Focus mode** (the button on the timer, or `#focus`) hides everything except one task, an "I will…" line, and the timer. Turn on **Sound: brown noise** if it helps. Press Esc to leave.
- After each session, rate it **Rough / OK / Flow**. Review uses these ratings to show your focus quality.
- To add a task quickly from anywhere, press `N`. For example, `Call Rob re Calendly 15m tomorrow @10am #site-launches !high` sets the date, a time block, the estimate, the project and the priority in one line.

## Every evening: shut down (2 minutes)

Use the **End-of-day shutdown** panel on Today:

1. Rate your energy.
2. Write one line on what went well.
3. Set tomorrow's highlight.
4. Press **Move unfinished to tomorrow**.
5. Press **Shutdown complete**.

Your score for the day (40 highlight · 30 focus vs plan · 15 habits · 15 tasks) appears in the greeting line.

## Sunday: review (5 minutes)

**Review (7)** shows the week's daily scores, focus by day and project, highlights hit, habit consistency, focus quality, and the season summary. It also shows whether your weekly three actually served the season. Fill in the three reflection boxes, then plan Monday.

## End of a season

**Seasons → Close season** asks what the season taught you and keeps the answer in *Past seasons*. Then start the next season with the goals that deserve the next 12 weeks.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `N` | Quick-add a task |
| `1`–`8` | Today, Week, Tasks, Goals, Seasons, Habits, Review, Settings |
| `Space` | Start or pause the timer |
| `Esc` | Leave focus mode |
